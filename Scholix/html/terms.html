USING SCHOLIX

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 22, 2026

These terms describe appropriate use of the current Scholix school records system. By signing in, you agree to follow these terms and your school’s rules for handling student information.

Authorized accounts

Use only the account provided or approved for you by the school. Keep your password private, sign out when using a shared device, and promptly tell a school administrator if you believe someone else has accessed your account.

Use records responsibly

  • Access student and school information only when needed for your assigned role.
  • Do not copy, share, publish, or use records for an unauthorized purpose.
  • Enter accurate information and upload only documents the school is authorized to collect.
  • Follow school instructions for attendance, grades, fees, document review, retention, and deletion.

Role permissions

Scholix presents tools according to the signed-in account’s role. Administrators manage school-wide records and settings; teachers work within their assigned scope; students can view the information made available to their own account. Do not attempt to bypass these limits or use another person’s account.

Current browser-based version

The current static version stores records and uploaded requirement files in the browser on the device being used. It does not provide cross-device synchronization or a central backup. Browser resets, storage limits, or clearing site data may remove information. Do not treat the browser copy as the school’s sole official record.

Availability and changes

Features may change as the school configures or updates its Scholix deployment. The school is responsible for communicating any deployment-specific procedures and for maintaining appropriate backups and access controls.

Questions

For help with your account, access permissions, or school records, contact your school administrator or the person responsible for your Scholix deployment.

These terms describe the current browser-based version. A school using a hosted or integrated deployment should provide any additional terms that apply to that service.
