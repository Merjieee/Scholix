Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This page explains how information is handled in the current Scholix browser-based school records system. Scholix is designed to help authorized school users organize student profiles, attendance, grades, fee status, and school requirement documents.

Information in the system

Depending on the account and features used, records may include student names, roll numbers, grades, guardian contact details, attendance, academic results, fee status, account profile details, and uploaded school documents.

How this version stores information

In the current static version, roster, section, profile, and preference data are stored in this browser using local storage. Uploaded requirement files are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. The records are not synced to another device by this version and may be lost if browser data is cleared or the device is reset.

Scholix pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. That request may share standard connection information with Google, such as the browser’s network address.

How information is used

Information is used to display and manage school records, calculate summaries, support role-specific views, review submitted documents, and save changes made in the system. The interface limits what each role can see or change.

Access and safeguarding

Use only an account assigned to you by the school, and keep its credentials private. The current browser-based version provides role-based interface controls but does not replace server-side access controls, secure backups, or a school’s data-protection procedures.

Retention and deletion

In this version, information remains in the browser until it is changed or browser storage is cleared. School administrators should follow the school’s retention and deletion policies when managing records.

For school deployments

Before entering real student or guardian information, confirm that the school has approved the deployment and its storage, access, backup, and retention arrangements. A hosted or connected deployment may handle data differently and should provide a deployment-specific privacy notice.

Questions about a record or a school’s use of Scholix should be directed to the school administrator or the person responsible for the specific Scholix deployment.
