Common questions

Before you move the roster

Short answers to what registrars and IT leads ask before switching.

Can staff have a physical copy of the roster?

Yes. Export a spreadsheet with names, grades and guardian contacts; Scholix maps the columns and lists any rows it could not read so nothing is silently dropped.

What exactly can a teacher change?

Attendance for their own sections only. Student records stay read-only, and adding or deleting records is reserved for administrators.

Do students see the whole school?

No. A student session is scoped to that student's own profile, attendance and fee balance - other records are not reachable from the interface.

We run more than one campus. Does that work?

Yes. Campuses share the same roster standard while keeping their own sections and fee plans, so a transfer is a move rather than a re-entry.
